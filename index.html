How to use the LitBuy spreadsheet

  1. Match the source item. Open a HipoBuyFind listing and use its purchase options. If it offers a LitBuy link, match the product and selected variant against the seller listing before continuing.
  2. Compare the same variant. Choose the exact color and size before recording the item price. Save the seller link, measurements and variant so you can compare listings on equal terms.
  3. Check warehouse photos. If QC photos are available for your order, compare the received color and size against your order details. Ask about any visible defects or measurement differences before approving shipment.
  4. Get a shipping quote. Use the agent’s quote for your destination and parcel. Compare packing options, weight and dimensions. Include any known fees or import charges in your estimate.

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list that helps shoppers compare finds and trace them back to seller listings. This independent guide features products listed on HipoBuyFind.

Does the USD price include delivery?

The USD figures are approximate conversions of the listed item price. Use the current variant price, exchange rate, shipping quote and any applicable fees to calculate the amount you may pay.

Are listing photos and QC photos the same?

Listing photos show how the seller presents a product. Warehouse QC photos, when available, show the item received for a specific order. Check that the photos match your order and chosen variant.

What if a product link no longer works?

Search HipoBuyFind by the item ID or product name. If you find another listing, recheck the seller, size chart, price and purchase options before ordering.
